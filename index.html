Physics Model

How a Hohmann transfer works

A Hohmann transfer moves a spacecraft between two circular orbits using an elliptical transfer orbit. The first impulse changes the spacecraft's speed to enter the ellipse. The second impulse circularizes the orbit at the destination.

Equation

Vis-viva equation

v = √[ μ(2/r − 1/a) ]

This relates orbital speed to gravitational parameter μ, current radius r, and orbit semi-major axis a.

Assumptions

What this model ignores

Atmospheric drag, plane changes, finite-duration burns, third-body effects, and orbital perturbations are outside this simplified model.

Engineering Use

Why delta-v matters

Delta-v is a mission's velocity-change requirement. It strongly influences propellant needs, vehicle mass, and mission feasibility.
